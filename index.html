JARVIS BUSINESS

A private, single-owner Mac application operated by Asilbek. It organizes a bounded business workflow for the HVAC Follow-up Queue: business prospect records, approved-template conversations, explicit discovery answers, order records and staff-reviewed draft queues.

This application is for its owner's Gmail account only. It does not offer public sign-up or access to other people's Google accounts. It is not an unrestricted negotiator or a guarantee of revenue.

Google integration

The owner can authorize Gmail read-only and sending scopes. The application checks its own account connection, processes business conversation replies, honors opt-outs and sends only within an explicitly approved exact audience, templates, time window and limits. It cannot delete Gmail messages and does not fetch attachments or execute email instructions.

Payment processing is not enabled. No merchant account, settlement destination or Apple Pay/Google Pay checkout has been connected.
