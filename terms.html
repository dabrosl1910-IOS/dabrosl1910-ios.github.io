Application use and service boundaries

JARVIS BUSINESS is a private application for its owner, Asilbek. It has no public signup, payment activation or automatic subscription. This page describes operating boundaries; it is not a new buyer order, signed agreement or permission to charge anyone.

Authorized operation

The owner reviews real sender/postal details, contact eligibility, exact recipients, all templates, caps/expiry and data handling before separately activating a run. Published contact information is not treated as prior consent. Opt-outs suppress pending/future messages. Unknown submissions pause without automatic replay. Exceptions and ambiguous conversations require owner review.

Single initial offer

The sole proposed commercial test is HVAC Follow-up Queue: $150 USD, 14 days, up to six submitted records total, one refresh of that same batch and at most 150 delivery minutes. Actual discovery evidence, an exact buyer acceptance, independently confirmed payment and an agreed minimal-data process are separate requirements before delivery. No outcome, recovered revenue, appointment or repeat purchase is promised.

Outputs are drafts for contractor staff review. JARVIS does not send to the contractor's end customers or access its CRM. Sending a research YES or claiming a payment by email does not create a settled payment. A separate actual buyer agreement governs any commercial engagement; this page is not a checkout.

Availability and finance

The local Mac/app must remain awake and online. Gmail authorization and provider availability can interrupt operation. There is no promise of endless unattended activity. All spending, transfers and refunds require explicit owner approval. Payment processing and settlement are not enabled.
