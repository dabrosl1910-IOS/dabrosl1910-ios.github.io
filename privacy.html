Privacy and data practices

Prepared 13 November 2026 for the owner-operated JARVIS BUSINESS application. Contact: asilbek.jarvis@gmail.com.

Access and purpose

Only the owner's approved Gmail account can connect through this application. Gmail read-only and send permissions support account verification, an explicitly authorized self-test, approved business conversations, opt-out processing and in-scope staff-review service delivery. Inbox message additions may be parsed transiently in memory. A separately approved Spam/Trash/Archive opt-out scan checks exact business sender metadata before reading a body; other senders' bodies are not read through that scan.

Storage and disclosure

Business recipient/source records, sender contact/postal address, approved outgoing templates, order/invoice records, message references, counters, decisions and hashes are stored in local SQLite and its backups. Inbound message bodies and customer-level service rows/generated CSV are not stored there. Service rows and CSV are processed transiently in application memory, then sent only to the same approved business buyer. Gmail itself retains incoming and sent emails until manually deleted by the account owner.

Durable authorization credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Short-lived access tokens may remain in process memory until expiry if updating an already readable Keychain credential is denied. There is no alternative plaintext credential file. Message data and credentials are not sold, shared with advertising platforms, or passed to language models by this workflow. Google processes Gmail traffic as the mail provider; approved outgoing recipients receive the messages addressed to them. This static page has no form, analytics script or payment collection. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub serves the files and logs visitor IP addresses for security under its own privacy practices.

Minimal service data

Customer names, phone/email, addresses, payment details, attachments and free-text customer notes are not accepted service fields. The buyer must agree to opaque references and enumerated flags, Gmail retention until manual deletion, and contractor staff review. Staff checks eligibility/facts and performs any end-customer sending through its own tools.

Retention and control

The owner controls the local installation, SQLite/backups and Gmail account. There is no automatic deletion schedule for business records/backups or Gmail messages. Disconnecting in JARVIS removes local saved connection credentials; Google access can also be revoked in Google Account security settings. Disconnect does not delete mailbox content, existing business records or backups. Deletion and backup retention need separate owner-controlled maintenance. Contact the owner for questions or requests; this page does not claim an automated privacy-request service.

Limited use

JARVIS BUSINESS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used for the user-facing workflow described here, not to train generalized AI models or create unrelated advertising profiles.
